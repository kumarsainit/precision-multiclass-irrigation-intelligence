Algorithm Logic

Leaf-wise Growth

LightGBM grows trees leaf-wise (best-first) rather than level-wise. This results in significantly lower loss and better fit for high-variance soil moisture data.

GOSS Optimization

Gradient-based One-Side Sampling allows the model to focus on data points with larger gradients, improving training speed without sacrificing accuracy.

Deployment Strategy

The trained model is loaded from the saved model artifact and served through the Flask prediction API.
