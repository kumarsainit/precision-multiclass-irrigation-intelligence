Second-Order Optimization

XGBoost utilizes both first-order gradient direction and second-order curvature information from the multi-class log-loss objective to calculate optimal leaf node weights with high computational efficiency.

Tree Regularization

Directly penalizes tree complexity using combined L1 and L2 regularization penalties, preventing overfitting on noisy soil sensor and atmospheric telemetry.
